About Momentum

A lot of college students spend more time on their phones than they'd like, especially in the evenings, and it can leave them feeling more alone. Momentum is our attempt to help: small daily habits, short lessons about how phones and apps compete for your attention, and real activities around Provo where you can meet people in person.

Momentum is being built by a team of students at Brigham Young University as a project for an information systems class. It is not a company and is not run, sponsored, or endorsed by BYU. It's free, it has no ads, and it's still being built, so some features are unfinished.

Momentum is for people 18 and older.

Where it's stored

  • Supabase runs our database and logins. Database rules (called row level security) make sure each person can only read and change what they're allowed to.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website's files. It doesn't receive anything you type into Momentum.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's fonts, so Google can see that your browser loaded them.
  • Your browser keeps a few things on your device, like your sign-in and some settings.

What we don't do: no ads, no selling or sharing your information, and no tracking or analytics tools. If we ever add a feature that sends information to another service, we'll update this page and tell you before it's turned on.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile any time in Settings.
  • Delete your posts and archive your habits whenever you like.
  • To delete your account and everything in it, or to ask us anything, email cbs706@byu.edu.

Meeting up safely

  • Meet in public places, especially the first time.
  • Tell a friend where you're going and when you'll be back.
  • Trust your gut. It's always okay to leave or skip an activity.
  • If someone makes you feel unsafe, contact local authorities first, then let us know.
