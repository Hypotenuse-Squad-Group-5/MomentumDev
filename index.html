Everything points you off the screen

Momentum isn't another feed. Each part is built to help you put the phone down and do something real.

Daily habits

Pick a few small habits, check in each day, and watch your streak grow.

Short lessons

Five-minute reads on how apps grab your attention and what actually helps.

Things to do

Find activities around Provo, or post your own, from game nights to hikes.

Real friends

Commit to plans, show up, and stay connected with the people you meet.

How it works

Create an account

Just a few basics, like your name and email. Momentum is for people 18 and older.

Start small

Choose a habit or two and read your first lesson. It takes about five minutes.

Show up

Join an activity, meet people in person, and spend time together offline.

Who's behind this

A class project, built in the open

Momentum is being built by a team of BYU students for an information systems class. It isn't a company, and it isn't run or endorsed by BYU. We built it because we know how easy it is to lose an evening to scrolling.

  • We only ask for what the app needs.
  • Your email is never shown to other people, and your last name is only visible to your friends.
  • No ads, and we never sell your data.
